ARCHITECTURE

Physical layer
16 nearby frequency pairs between roughly 2.6–9.2 kHz. Each pair is nudged by only a fraction of a dB.
Differential code
Every 200 ms code is sent as +pattern then −pattern. Subtracting the two halves cancels much of the programme's natural spectral bias.
Frame
0–200 ms preamble, 200–400 ms minute ID, 400–600 ms second ID, 600–1000 ms untouched audio.
Capacity
Minute 0–59 + second 0–59 gives absolute show position for a one-hour programme without streaming LED values.
Clock
SHOW_TIME = RATE × RECEIVER_AUDIO_CLOCK + OFFSET. Anchors discipline the clock; local audio time free-runs during holdover.
Important
This is an open experimental spectral-patchwork design, not Evixar Another Track and not the audiowmark implementation.

RECOMMENDED FIRST TEST

1

2

3
Receiver 30–50 cm from the speaker; volume around normal listening level.
4
After lock is stable, lower watermark strength toward 0.5–0.8 dB until A/B becomes perceptually negligible.
